### 6.2. Abstract & Scientific Problem Formulation
Diagnosing foliar plant diseases early and reliably is critical to food security and precision agriculture. Deploying vision foundation models to agricultural edge devices faces two persistent barriers:
1. **Extreme Data Scarcity:** Novel crop pathogens lack large-scale annotated training corpora, requiring Few-Shot Learning (FSL).
2. **Strict Edge Resource Constraints:** Web-scale ViT backbones exceed the memory, compute, and thermal budgets of field scouting robots, UAVs, and mobile phones.

**BioDINO-Edge** introduces an adaptive dual-foundation paradigm uniting:
* A lightweight self-supervised spatial patch extractor: **DINOv2 Small** (22.1M parameters, 384-d feature representation, 21.12 ms on CPU).
* An organismal vision-language foundation model: **BioCLIP ViT-B/16** (86.6M parameters, 512-d feature representation capturing fine-grained biological phylogenetic hierarchies).

### 6.3. Key Methodological Innovations & Mathematical Formulations

#### 1. Dual-Foundation Latent Manifold Concatenation ($d = 896$)
Fuses localized spatial patch features with macro-organismal semantic priors into a unified 896-dimensional representation:
$$\mathbf{z} = [\mathbf{f}_{\text{bio}} \parallel \mathbf{f}_{\text{dino}}] \in \mathbb{R}^{896}$$
where $\mathbf{f}_{\text{bio}} \in \mathbb{R}^{512}$ and $\mathbf{f}_{\text{dino}} \in \mathbb{R}^{384}$.

#### 2. Regularized Fisher-Discriminant Alignment via Analytical Ledoit-Wolf Covariance Shrinkage
In the few-shot regime ($N = 440$ multi-view support vectors, $d = 896$), the empirical within-class scatter matrix $\mathbf{S}_W$ is mathematically singular ($\text{rank}(\mathbf{S}_W) \le N - K < d$). Instead of backpropagation or heuristic regularization, the framework derives analytical Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage in closed form:
$$\hat{\mathbf{\Sigma}} = (1 - \rho^*) \mathbf{S}_W + \rho^* \nu \mathbf{I}_{896}$$
where $\nu = \frac{1}{d} \text{Tr}(\mathbf{S}_W)$ is the shrinkage target and $\rho^*$ is the analytically optimal shrinkage intensity derived from sample second moments.  
* **Inference Overhead:** Evaluating the resulting Fisher linear discriminant requires only **9.8K FLOPs** and executes in **$< 0.003$ ms** ($> 10,000$ FPS), adding zero effective latency to edge microprocessors.

#### 3. Purified Taxonomic Domain Synthesis
Zero-shot linguistic prompts often include negative tokens (e.g., *"leaf with no spots"*), which unintentionally pull healthy leaf representations toward diseased semantic clusters. The method enforces affirmative botanical prompts describing leaf morphology (*"intact smooth leaf blade, vibrant mesophyll"*), completely preventing cross-class semantic leakage.

#### 4. Soft-Anchored Transductive Self-Calibration
To resolve the $k$-shot prototype shift between the few labeled support examples and streaming query distributions without backpropagation:
* Evaluates unlabeled query streams transductively.
* Imposes $3\times$ heavier anchoring weight on confirmed ground-truth support prototypes.
* Merges high-confidence pseudo-labeled queries ($C(x) \ge 0.85$) into centroid updates in $< 22$ ms on edge CPUs.

#### 5. Predictive Shannon Margin Uncertainty Gating
Routes query instances dynamically using confidence $C(x)$:
$$C(x) = (p_{(1)} - p_{(2)}) \cdot (1 - H_{\text{norm}}(p))$$
where $p_{(1)}$ and $p_{(2)}$ are top-1 and top-2 posterior probabilities, and $H_{\text{norm}}(p) = -\sum p_i \ln p_i / \ln K$ is normalized Shannon entropy.
* **Tier-1 Fast Path ($C(x) \ge 0.35$):** Directly classified by DINOv2 Small in 21.12 ms ($> 45$ FPS). Resolves $> 88\%$ of field queries.
* **Tier-2 Gated Deep Path ($C(x) < 0.35$):** Ambiguous or heavily occluded lesions are routed to the full 896-d BioDINO consensus manifold.

#### 6. Dual-Regime Saliency & Attention Sink Suppression
Standard un-fine-tuned ViTs suffer from "attention sink" phenomena, erroneously pooling attention weights at high-contrast, non-pathological leaf petiole junctions.
* Regularized Fisher-LDA precision projection ($\hat{\mathbf{\Sigma}}_W^{-1}$) suppresses petiole vegetative feature variance by **$> 31\times$** ($\sigma^2 = 12.8 \to 0.41$).
* Cleanly restores focus and saliency onto active necrotic fungal pustules and lesion rings.

#### 7. Post-Hoc Temperature Scaling Uncertainty Calibration
Naive multimodal posteriors suffer from overconfidence or diffuse posteriors. Applying parameter-free global temperature scaling ($T = 0.50$):
$$p_{\text{cal}} = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{\log(p + \epsilon)}{T}\right)$$
reduces Expected Calibration Error (**ECE**) from **37.97%** to **0.81%** on 9,330 unseen test queries, with Maximum Calibration Error (**MCE**) kept under 6.09%.
